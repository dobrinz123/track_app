Pick the track. Or teach it a new one.

OUT-LAP

The learn lap maps where you actually drive, so corners and sectors line up with your car, not a generic map.

S1 · LIVE

The next corner, before you reach it.

Corner number, how tight it is on a 1–6 scale, a target speed and the metres left. Everything you need at a glance, nothing to read at speed.

S1 · LIVE

Then one word: Brake.

Switch the voice on and it keeps to three callouts made for beginners: “Brake hard” for hairpins, “Brake”, and “Lift” for the fast ones. Your eyes stay on the track.

Voice callouts are optional. You turn them on in settings.

S2 · PITS

In the pits: the three corners costing you most.

Stop guessing between sessions. The Pit view ranks where you are losing time and shows your brake points lap by lap, so you change one thing, not ten.

Part of the optional trackday suggestions. Any suggestion stays inside what your own clean laps have already done.

S3 · AFTER

After the session: every corner, lap by lap.

Time lost, consistency, entry and exit speed for each corner, measured against your own best clean lap. Share a one-page report in English or Romanian.
